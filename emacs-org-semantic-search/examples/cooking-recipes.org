* Breakfast Recipes                                            :breakfast:

** Pancakes                                               :breakfast:easy:

Fluffy pancakes are a classic breakfast staple.

*** Ingredients

- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup milk
- 1 egg
- 2 tablespoons melted butter

*** Instructions

1. Mix dry ingredients in a bowl
2. Whisk wet ingredients separately
3. Combine wet and dry ingredients
4. Cook on griddle until bubbles form
5. Flip and cook other side

** Scrambled Eggs                                      :breakfast:protein:

Quick and easy protein-rich breakfast.

- Beat eggs with milk
- Cook in butter over medium heat
- Stir continuously until done
- Season with salt and pepper

* Dinner Recipes                                                  :dinner:

** Spaghetti Carbonara                                    :dinner:pasta:italian:

Classic Italian pasta dish.

*** Ingredients

- Spaghetti
- Eggs
- Parmesan cheese
- Pancetta or bacon
- Black pepper

*** Cooking Steps

1. Cook pasta according to package
2. Fry pancetta until crispy
3. Mix eggs and cheese
4. Combine hot pasta with egg mixture
5. Add pancetta and pepper

** Grilled Salmon                                     :dinner:fish:healthy:

Nutritious and delicious grilled salmon.

*** Marinade

- Olive oil
- Lemon juice
- Garlic
- Herbs (dill, parsley)

*** Grilling

1. Marinate salmon for 30 minutes
2. Preheat grill to medium-high
3. Grill skin-side down for 5-6 minutes
4. Flip and grill 3-4 minutes more

* Desserts                                                        :dessert:

** Chocolate Chip Cookies                               :dessert:baking:sweet:

Classic homemade cookies.

*** Cookie Dough

- 2 cups flour
- 1 cup butter
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 3/4 cup white sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 cups chocolate chips

*** Baking

1. Cream butter and sugars
2. Add eggs and vanilla
3. Mix in flour
4. Fold in chocolate chips
5. Bake at 375°F for 10-12 minutes
